Research

Research Areas

Research at the intersection of wireless communications, sensing, optimization, and distributed artificial intelligence.

Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC)

Joint waveform, beamforming, sensing, localization, target tracking, and communication design for future 6G networks.

Cell-Free Massive MIMO

Distributed access-point architectures, coordinated transmission, scalable processing, and robust multi-user optimization.

Semantic Communications

Adaptive semantic coding, channel-quality prediction, reliability-aware transmission, semantic utility, and queue-aware control.

Federated Learning & AirComp

Wireless federated learning, over-the-air aggregation, learning-oriented resource allocation, and task-oriented communication.

UAV-Enabled Wireless Networks

Trajectory design, mobile access points, energy efficiency, mURLLC, and sensing-assisted wireless optimization.

AI-Native 6G Networks

Prediction, control, learning, and optimization mechanisms that jointly adapt communication and AI tasks to network conditions.
